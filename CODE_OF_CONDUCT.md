# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and harassment-free
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other
protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and the rights of photographers and subjects.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security reporting page](https://github.com/Rohit22014/react-photo-details-lightbox/security/advisories/new).
Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and handled as fairly and promptly as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
